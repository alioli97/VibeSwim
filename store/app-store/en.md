## Description

SwimVibe gives you a complete pool workout in seconds, sized to your session.

YOUR DISTANCE OR YOUR TIME
Choose the meters you want to swim, or the minutes you have before you need to leave the pool. SwimVibe finds the workout closest to your distance, or the longest one that fits in your time.

A COMPLETE PLAN
Every workout comes with a warm-up, a main set and a cool-down, written the way swimmers read them: 2x200 medley, 300 kick, 200 backstroke… Filter by difficulty (beginner, intermediate or advanced), and tap "Generate another" if you fancy something different.

LOG IT AND SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Tap "Finish & Log" when you are done.
• Set a weekly goal in meters and watch the bar fill up.
• See your last 8 weeks, your streak, your totals and this month against last month.
• Workouts you swam in the last 14 days are avoided, so your training stays varied.
• Star the workouts you love to find them again.
• Share a workout with your training partner.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your history, goal and favorites are stored only on your iPhone. Export a backup file whenever you want, and import it on a new phone.

In English, Spanish and Catalan.

The workouts are general suggestions, not medical advice. Swim at your own level and check with a doctor if you have a health condition.
